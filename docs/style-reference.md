# Style reference

From the pilot (b001 = script/archive/f00, the prologue), translated by the controller. Match it.

## Voices
| Who | JP markers | English devices |
|---|---|---|
| `{p}` (player, boy) | 僕, だよ, ～かい？, soft stutters (い いや) | plain friendly "I", "Hey!", "Geez", stutters "N-no" |
| `{s}` (girlfriend) | あたし, ～わよ, ～じゃない, ちょっとぉ | sassy, exclamatory: "Honestly!", "Hey! Are you even listening!?", "No excuses!" |
| Honey Kisaragi | あたし, ～わ, ～のよ, パパ | bright, a little girlish: "Lucky!", "Papa!", "Uh-huh." |
| Akira Fudo (early) | 僕, ～んだ, ちゃん/君 | earnest, a bit unsure: "Geez, that Ryo..."; keeps -chan / -kun |
| Ryo Asuka | 俺, terse imperatives, 来い | clipped and commanding: "Come." No softeners |
| Juzo Kabuto, old men | わし, ～じゃ, ～のだ, わっはっは | grand and hearty: "I am...", "Wahahaha!", "I'd love to see it!" |
| Villains | 貴様, ～ぞ, ～め | theatrical: "Fools!", "You'll pay for this!" |

## Examples (JP -> EN)
- いいわけ無用！ もう知らない！ -> No excuses! I'm through with you!
- だって 急に呼び出すんだもん… -> But you called me out of nowhere...
- か 可愛い… -> Sh-she's cute...
- いい男だけど なんだか 頼りない感じの子ね… -> He's good-looking, but he seems kind of a pushover...
- 俺の用のほうが 重要だ 来い -> My business is more important. Come.
- 神となり人類を救う事も 悪魔となり世界を滅ぼすことも 自由だ！！ -> It can become a god and save mankind, or
  become a devil and destroy the world! The choice is yours!
- 明君チームと ハニーチームに 別れるの -> we split into Team Akira and Team Honey
- Corrected: 牧村さん家 first came out as "Mr. Makimura's place"; it is the household he boards with -> "the
  Makimuras', where I board" (no honorific needed: さん here is part of 〜さん家, "the X family's house").
- `{g}を手に入れた` -> `Got {g}!`; `{g}円 手に入れた` -> `Got {g} yen!` (the yen is the game's currency).

## Conventions
- Write sentences; the tool wraps dialogue. Keep a blank line (`\n\n`) where the JP has one (a new page); add one
  only to separate a beat, never to shorten a box.
- 「...」 quoted messages -> double quotes. Trailing ！！ -> "!!", ！？ -> "!?".
- Screams: きゃ～！ -> "Kyaaa!", うわ～！！ -> "Whoaaa!!", う う～ん… -> "...Uhh... nnn...".
